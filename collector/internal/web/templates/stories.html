A note on this edition

Profiles are saved only in this browser for this snapshot and included in same-origin recommendation requests. Matches use content and explicit interests, not an account or historical-user learning.

Every story belongs to an archived snapshot. Dates are absolute, shown in UTC, and may be collection dates when a publication date was not stored.

Topic circles are unsupervised content groups, not verified editorial categories. A summary or headline is not a substitute for the original reporting. External pages may have changed or disappeared.

Local to this snapshot: up to 50 likes, 50 not-interested choices, and the last 2,000 viewed articles. No telemetry is sent by this page.
